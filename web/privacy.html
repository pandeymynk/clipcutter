Privacy policy

Last updated 27 November 2026

ClipCutter is built to keep your data on your own device. This policy explains what that means for the app and for this website.

The ClipCutter app

  • Your videos stay on your device. Downloads, transcripts and finished clips are saved on your computer or phone. We don't upload them anywhere.
  • No account, no tracking. The app has no sign-up and doesn't send analytics or usage data to us.
  • What the app connects to: the video sites you paste links from (to download the video and its captions), Hugging Face (to download the speech model once), and PyPI (to update the video downloader when you click "Update downloader").
  • Optional AI key. If you add an AI key (NVIDIA, Groq, OpenRouter, Gemini or another provider), it's stored only on your device, and the clip's transcript text is sent to that provider to write the hook. That provider's own privacy policy applies. Without a key, nothing is sent.

This website

  • clipcutter.in doesn't use cookies, analytics or ads, and has no forms.
  • Our host, Vercel, keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security and reliability.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts. Download buttons go to GitHub, and video links go to YouTube; their own privacy policies apply there.

Children

ClipCutter isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
