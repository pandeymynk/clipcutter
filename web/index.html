Questions

Is ClipCutter really free?

Yes. It runs on your own device, so there are no server costs to pass on to you: no subscription, no credits and no watermark.

Do I need coding skills or an API key?

No. Install it and paste a link. Clips, captions and hooks work out of the box. A free AI key (NVIDIA, Groq, OpenRouter or Gemini) is optional, only if you want AI-written hooks.

Which devices does it run on?

Windows 10/11 (64-bit), Macs with an Apple chip (M1–M4), and Android 10+ phones (beta). 8 GB RAM is enough on a computer; on Android, 6 GB or more works best.

How long does a clip take?

Most of the time goes into downloading the video. A 1h 43m GTA 6 stream took about 15 minutes on a normal laptop, and each clip rendered in under 30 seconds. Phones are slower.

Does it work for Hindi streams?

Yes. It understands Hindi and English hype words, and Hindi captions can be shown in Hinglish (Roman letters).

Can I edit a clip?

Yes. Change the hook text or fix any caption word, and the clip re-renders in a few seconds. Title and post caption are editable too.

Will I earn money with it?

ClipCutter makes the clips. Earnings come from clipping campaigns and depend on views, the campaign's rate and its rules. There's no guarantee, and fake views get accounts banned.

Can I clip any video?

Only clip content you have permission to use, such as a campaign's own footage or a streamer who allows clipping.
