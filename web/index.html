Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. ClipCutter runs on your own computer, so there are no server costs to pass on: no subscription, no credits, no watermark.

Do I need an API key or any coding?

No. Clips, captions and hooks work out of the box. If you want AI-written hooks, you can paste a free key (NVIDIA, Groq, OpenRouter or Gemini) into the app. That part is optional.

What device do I need?

Windows 10/11 (64-bit), a Mac with an Apple chip, or an Android 10+ phone. 8 GB RAM is enough. Keep a few GB free: the app downloads the video once and cuts clips from it.

How long does it take?

Most of the time goes into downloading. A 1h 43m GTA 6 stream took about 15 minutes on a normal laptop, and each clip rendered in under 30 seconds.

Is there an Android app?

Yes, in beta. The Android app runs fully offline on the phone: it downloads the video, finds the moments and renders clips itself. Clips take longer than on a laptop, and a phone with 6 GB RAM or more works best.

Can I clip any video?

Only clip content you have permission to use, such as a campaign's own footage or a streamer who allows clipping. Follow each campaign's rules.
